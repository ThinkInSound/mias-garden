# Mia's Garden

Let us pick apples naked under the moonlight.

Let us feast on the flesh of the new born plum.

Let us dance to the music of our children.

Let us bray and whine and be calmed by each other’s caress.

Let us laugh and tell stories until our tongues get tired and our cheeks get bruised.

Let us touch and feel the breathlessness of falling through the sky.
